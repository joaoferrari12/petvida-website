Services

What we do, and when you need it

Six areas of care, one team. If you are not sure which one you need, call and we will point you the right way.

Clinical examinations

Thorough check-ups that catch problems early, with time to answer every question you bring.

  • Full physical exam and weight tracking
  • Senior pet wellness panels
  • Nutrition and behaviour guidance

Vaccination and prevention

Full vaccine schedules plus flea, tick and worm control to keep your pet protected year-round.

  • Rabies, distemper, parvovirus, leptospirosis
  • Puppy and kitten first-year plans
  • Reminders before each dose is due

Diagnostics and imaging

X-rays, ultrasound and an in-house lab, so you get answers the same day instead of next week.

  • Digital radiography and ultrasound
  • Blood, urine and fecal tests on site
  • Endoscopy for the hard cases

Surgery

From spaying and neutering to orthopedics, with careful pre-op assessment and post-op monitoring.

  • Routine spay and neuter
  • Soft tissue and orthopedic procedures
  • Pain management and recovery plans

Dentistry

Cleanings, extractions and periodontal care that protect more than just teeth.

  • Ultrasonic cleaning under anesthesia
  • Dental X-rays
  • Home-care coaching for owners

Therapies and treatments

Long-term care for chronic conditions, plus physiotherapy and acupuncture when they help.

  • Diabetes and kidney disease follow-up
  • Physiotherapy after surgery
  • Acupuncture for chronic pain
