Be our Partner! Learn about: PetVida Loyalty Plan

Welcome to the PetVida Loyalty Plan! Our exclusive membership program offers unparalleled benefits, savings, and conveniences for pet owners committed to providing the best care for their furry friends. Here’s how you can benefit:

Membership Tiers

Silver Paw Plan

Annual Fee: $99

Benefits:

  • 10% Discount on all routine veterinary services.
  • Complimentary Annual Wellness Exam for one pet.
  • Priority Booking for appointments.
  • Access to Members-Only Events and Workshops.

Gold Paw Plan

Annual Fee: $199

Benefits:

  • 15% Discount on all routine veterinary services.
  • Complimentary Annual Wellness Exam and Vaccination Package for one pet.
  • Two Complimentary Nail Trimmings per year.
  • Priority Booking and Extended Hours Access.
  • Free Telemedicine Consultations for minor health concerns.
  • Welcome Package with premium pet care products.

Platinum Paw Plan

Annual Fee: $299

Benefits:

  • 20% Discount on all veterinary services, including surgeries and dental care.
  • Complimentary Annual Wellness Exam and Comprehensive Vaccination Package for two pets.
  • Four Complimentary Grooming Sessions per year.
  • Priority Booking, Extended Hours Access, and Emergency Care.
  • Unlimited Telemedicine Consultations.
  • Free Microchipping for new pets.
  • Exclusive Access to PetVida’s Health and Wellness Webinars.
  • Loyalty Points for every dollar spent, redeemable for future services and products.

Additional Member Benefits

  • Exclusive Discounts: Enjoy special discounts on pet food, medications, and supplies available at our clinic.
  • Pet Health Tracker: Access to our online portal where you can track your pet’s health records, vaccination schedules, and upcoming appointments.
  • Pet Birthday Perks: Celebrate your pet’s special day with a complimentary treat and toy.
  • Referral Rewards: Earn additional loyalty points for every new client you refer to PetVida.
  • Personalized Care Plans: Receive tailored health care plans for your pet’s specific needs and lifestyle.
  • Emergency Hotline: Access to a 24/7 emergency hotline for urgent medical advice and support.

How to Enroll

  • Visit Our Clinic: Speak to our friendly reception staff to choose the best plan for you and your pet.
  • Online Enrollment: Sign up through our website by filling out a quick registration form and selecting your preferred membership tier.
  • Phone Registration: Call our clinic and our team will assist you with the enrollment process.

Why Join PetVida Loyalty Plan?

  • Cost Savings: Enjoy significant savings on routine and advanced veterinary services.
  • Peace of Mind: Ensure your pet receives regular and comprehensive care.
  • Convenience: Benefit from priority booking, extended hours, and emergency access.
  • Community: Be part of a caring and knowledgeable pet community with access to exclusive events and resources.

Joining the PetVida Loyalty Plan ensures you provide your pet with the best care possible while enjoying significant savings and exclusive benefits. Sign up today and let us partner with you in your pet’s journey to a long, healthy, and happy life.
